How to book the Goûter and Tête Rousse huts

On the normal route via the Goûter, the whole trip is planned around the huts. This is how we booked them for June 2026 and what we paid.

Marcin Wnuk, normal route via the Goûter, June 2026

In short

  • Book the Goûter first and fit the rest of the plan around its dates.
  • The FFCAM system needs every climber’s details and an ID scan, and you have 48 hours to pay the deposit.
  • Put Tête Rousse and the Goûter in one basket: the €5 booking fee is charged per basket.
  • Print the voucher. After 5 pm your booking can lapse unless you call the hut.
  • Our huts cost about €496 per person.

The Goûter first, everything else after

You can’t plan the normal route around the summit date you would like. First you check whether the huts have beds. We started with the Goûter and got two nights: 12 and 13 June. Only then did we book Tête Rousse for 11 June, then acclimatisation on Gran Paradiso with a night at Rifugio Vittorio Emanuele II, and finally transport and accommodation in the valley.

So the first question is “when does the Goûter have beds?” and only the second is “when do I want to go?”.

How the FFCAM system works

  • bookings on the normal route go through one system: montblanc.ffcam.fr,
  • you enter details for every climber and upload a scan of each person’s ID (ID card, passport or driving licence),
  • an FFCAM operator confirms the provisional booking by email, and from then you have 48 hours to finalise it and pay the deposit,
  • one booking covers at most 12 people, and bookings may not be resold,
  • the booking fee (€5) is charged per basket.

These were the rules in 2026. Check them again before you book.

Why two nights at the Goûter

A second night at the Goûter costs money, but it is a safety buffer. After the summit you come back to the same hut and descend the next day, instead of going straight down the rocks of the Aiguille du Goûter and through the Grand Couloir to Tête Rousse. If wind, weather or the team’s pace hold you up, you have more room to decide. The plan no longer depends on one perfect scenario.

What we paid

The booking price did not include food. We paid for meals and the rest of the overnight fee at the hut, in cash. Per person:

HutDepositAt the hutTotal
Tête Rousse€40€83€123
Goûter, first night€50€89€139
Goûter, second night€50€94€144
Rifugio Vittorio Emanuele IIabout €90 (estimate)

About €496 in total, not counting water, drinks and a beer after the descent. The amount paid at the hut is the remaining overnight fee (€15.80, or €20.80 for the second Goûter night) plus dinner and breakfast. A hut receipt therefore does not show the full price of the night, only the balance and the food. These are June 2026 prices, not a current price list.

Read the confirmation email to the end

The small print in the Tête Rousse email mattered:

  • print the voucher, because police on the normal route and staff at the hut entrance may check your booking together with your ID,
  • after 5 pm the booking may lapse unless you contact the hut, so if you are running late, call before then,
  • food and tourist tax are paid at the hut,
  • your own sleeping bag liner is compulsory (the hut sells them for €10).

I keep confirmations offline on my phone, in a shared team folder and on paper. Next time I would also keep one booking table from day one: hut, date, number of people, deposit, balance, who paid, booking number and the latest arrival time.
