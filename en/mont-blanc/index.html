Summit, 4,808 m, 13 June 2026

160 pages about everything that happens before the summit photo and after it. From a Facebook post and looking for a team, through hut bookings, a glacier course and acclimatisation on Gran Paradiso, to a night at the Goûter, waiting out the wind at the Vallot and the descent to the valley.

Who this ebook is for

For someone thinking about an independent trip to Mont Blanc who wants to understand the whole process: team, bookings, transport, budget, gear, acclimatisation, huts, summit day and the descent. For someone interested in all the small decisions that lead to the summit photo.

Who it is not for

If you are looking for a detailed topo describing every metre of the route, you will not find it here. This book will not replace a glacier course either, and it should not be the basis for deciding to climb without experience, gear and preparation. Mont Blanc is popular, but it is still a high and serious mountain.

From the author

For me this ebook does not start in Chamonix, at the Goûter or even with the hut bookings. It starts much earlier.

Mont Blanc was not a one-off idea or a moment of being dazzled by mountains. Looking back, I see a long road: from running with a map through the woods as a child, through an injury, marathons, trail races, the Tatras, courses and a serious health experience, to the concrete decision to come back to the idea of the highest peak in the Alps.
